# Scientific Documentation, Epistemic Classification & Limitations - EVOCURE v1.0.0

EVOCURE enforces strict scientific honesty across every calculation, visualization, export, and AI explanation (Sections 13.3, 21 & 27).

---

## 1. Mandatory Epistemic Classification (Section 27)

Every quantitative or categorical metric returned by EVOCURE carries an explicit `EpistemicCategory`:

1. **`Imported experimental observation`** (`EXPERIMENTAL_OBSERVATION`) - Measured literature/database values (e.g., curated GDSC2 / ChEMBL cell-line $\text{IC}_{50}$, bacterial MIC, or biochemical $\text{EC}_{50}$ entries with cited assay provenance).
2. **`Calculated physicochemical property`** (`CALCULATED_PHYSICOCHEMICAL`) - Deterministic chemical graph invariants computed via RDKit (exact molecular weight, formula, topological polar surface area, H-bond donors/acceptors, rotatable bonds, Crippen $\log P$).
3. **`Validated model prediction`** (`VALIDATED_MODEL_PREDICTION`) - Split-conformal calibrated QSAR predictions inside the verified Tanimoto applicability domain ($s_{\max} \ge 0.25$) evaluated under zero-leakage Bemis-Murcko scaffold splitting on strictly assay-homogeneous datasets.
4. **`Mechanistic simulation result`** (`MECHANISTIC_SIMULATION`) - Dynamical outputs arising directly from governing PDEs/ODEs and conservation rules (2D reaction-diffusion hypoxia/biofilm/zonation fractions, closed-box mass balance, clonal cell counts).
5. **`Exploratory model result`** (`EXPLORATORY_MODEL`) - Heuristic QSPR, out-of-domain estimates, Bliss/Loewe combination surfaces, internal 0-100 toxicological risk scores, and simplified 7-compartment PBPK systemic projections.
6. **`AI-generated hypothesis`** (`AI_HYPOTHESIS`) - Natural-language interpretations or experimental suggestions produced by the local Gemma 4 assistant.
7. **`Demonstrative result without external validation`** (`DEMONSTRATION_UNVALIDATED`) - Toy or Mode A artificial chemical life abstractions.
8. **`Insufficient data`** (`INSUFFICIENT_DATA`) & **`Not modeled`** (`UNMODELED`) - Explicitly displayed whenever molecules fall outside model bounds or when systemic physiology (e.g., hemoglobin, creatinine, ALT in 2D-only slice mode) is not modeled.

## 2. Hard Scientific & Ethical Guardrails

1. **Artificial Life $\neq$ Living Chemistry or Clinical Proof**:
   - Mode A (`ARTIFICIAL_LIFE`) models self-replication, catalysis, competition, and parasitism as an abstract computational system. Fitness or persistence in Mode A is **permanently quarantined** (`validate_for_biomedical_mode`) and never transferred into pharmacological efficacy claims.
2. **Strict Separation of Non-Comparable Assays (Section 13.3)**:
   - Reference records in `data/reference/pdac_reference_dataset.json` (90 compounds across `PDAC`, `NSCLC`, `AMR`, `MASLD`, and `CONTROL`) are strictly partitioned by `disease_id` and `assay_comparability_group`. Bacterial MICs (`AMR`) or hepatic nuclear receptor $\text{EC}_{50}$s (`MASLD`) are never pooled into human `PDAC` cellular cytotoxicity QSAR training.
3. **No LLM Authority Over Efficacy**:
   - Neither Gemma 4 nor neural Laya AI ever determines whether a molecule inhibits a disease target or is safe. All biological responses derive strictly from explicit mathematical/QSAR models with documented split-conformal uncertainty.
4. **Prohibition of Clinical Claims & Human Dosing**:
   - EVOCURE never outputs terms such as *"proven cure"*, *"safe for humans"*, or *"guaranteed clinical efficacy"*, and never recommends human doses, schedules, or self-administration.
5. **2D Tissue & Microenvironment Scope (`PDAC`, `NSCLC_KRAS`, `AMR_BACTERIAL`, `METABOLIC_MASLD`)**:
   - Captures spatial $\text{O}_2$/nutrient/lipid gradients, ECM/CAF desmoplasia, EPS biofilm hindrance, enzymatic drug hydrolysis, tripartite free-drug partitioning, Bliss/Loewe 2-drug interactions, and multifactorial subclonal resistance.
   - Does **not** capture full 3D organ vasculature, metastatic dissemination, adaptive systemic T-cell priming in lymph nodes, or human clinical trial pharmacokinetics.
6. **Deterministic Reproducibility Boundaries**:
   - Given an identical configuration and `PCG64` seed on the same CPU architecture and Python/NumPy build, simulations are deterministically reproducible and invariant to graphical FPS. Cross-architecture floating-point or GPU thread scheduling is not claimed to be bit-identical without verification.

7. **What Selection Optimises (Open vs Closed Loop)**:
   - The discovery pipeline's `tissue_in_fitness` flag decides whether the tumour microenvironment participates in selection. With it `False` (the default) the objective set is the five chemistry and prediction criteria, the microenvironment simulation runs only for the top ranked candidates, and it exerts **no selective pressure**: the search optimises a QSAR surrogate and the tissue figures are a readout. With it `True` every candidate that can be dosed is simulated and its growth inhibition becomes a sixth objective entering NSGA-II sorting and survivor selection.
   - Any statement that the platform "evolves compounds against a tumour" is accurate only in the closed-loop configuration, and every result must record which mode produced it. The published example report uses the closed loop and states so.
   - Even closed, this objective is not independent evidence. The tissue model's drug response is parameterised by the predicted $\text{IC}_{50}$, so the sixth objective is correlated with the first; it adds diffusion, uptake, hypoxia and clonal structure on top of a prediction rather than substituting a measurement for it. All arms in a generation share one seed, which pairs the comparison but leaves this the noisiest objective of the six.

8. **Descriptors Always Describe the Present Molecule**:
   - No code path may shift a parent compound's physicochemical descriptors by a fixed increment while leaving its structure unchanged and then record the result as a derivative. The coevolution engine performs an RDKit structural mutation and recomputes every descriptor from the resulting graph; when no valid variant is produced the parent is returned unchanged rather than relabelled. This is a correctness requirement, not a stylistic one, because fabricated descriptors propagate silently into the heuristic pharmacology and from there into tumour response. Pinned by `tests/level3_evolution/test_selection_loop.py`.

9. **Scale of the Search**:
   - Published runs evaluate on the order of $10^2$ unique structures across a handful of generations. Established molecular optimisation studies operate at $10^4$ to $10^6$ evaluations. Results here characterise the platform's behaviour, not the limits of what the method could find.

10. **Reference Drugs and Generated Structures Are Not Scored Comparably** (measured 2026-10-27, `scripts/investigate_potency_asymmetry.py`, output in `reports/potency_asymmetry.json`):
    - `PharmacologyEngine.predict_antitumor_activity` resolves potency by precedence: curated `reference_bioactivity` first, then a calibrated QSAR prediction if one is supplied, then an exploratory structure-property heuristic. The reporting path calls it **without** a QSAR prediction, so every newly generated structure falls to the heuristic while library reference drugs use curated experimental values.
    - The heuristic is $\text{IC}_{50} = \exp(3.0 - 3.2 f)$ with $f \in [0.05, 1.0]$ derived from MW, $\log P$ and QED. Swept across the plausible descriptor range it can only ever return **0.87 to 9.2 $\mu$M**. Gemcitabine's curated value is 0.045 $\mu$M, roughly **19 times below the most potent value the heuristic can emit for any molecule whatsoever**. No generated structure can be scored as potent as the positive control, irrespective of its chemistry.
    - Direct test: scoring gemcitabine itself through the heuristic (curated bioactivity withheld) gives 8.053 $\mu$M, 179 times weaker, and simulated growth inhibition collapses from 67.1% to -1.0%, which does not separate from the negative control. **The same molecule passes or fails purely according to the provenance of its potency value.**
    - Consequence for interpretation: a candidate failing to separate from the negative control in this configuration is **not** evidence that the candidate is inactive. It is a property of the scoring asymmetry. Published runs therefore carry a third control arm, the positive control re-scored through the heuristic, and that arm is the only fair comparator for generated structures.
    - The candidates are not inert in the model. A dose sweep (1, 5, 25, 100, 500 $\mu$M) shows the leading candidates rising from -7% to roughly 73% inhibition, while the negative control stays flat near -6% at every dose. They are underdosed relative to the potency assigned to them, not unresponsive.
    - Substituting the calibrated QSAR potency makes all five candidates separate from the negative control. This is **not** a better answer: that regressor's 95% conformal interval is about 4.2 log units, so it would make almost any structure look active. It demonstrates the tissue model's sensitivity to the potency input and nothing more.
    - **Binding constraint**: the platform currently has no potency estimator capable of supporting a comparison between a generated structure and a reference drug. Until one exists, tissue-level efficacy rankings of novel compounds should be read as exploratory ordering within the generated set, never as a comparison against approved therapeutics.

11. **Reported Validation Metrics Come From Repeated Grouped CV, Not One Split** (measured 2026-10-28, `scripts/investigate_potency_estimator.py`, output in `reports/potency_estimator_study.json`):
    - Earlier releases reported $R^2 = 0.32$ and a conformal half width of 4.16 log units from a **single** Bemis-Murcko scaffold split leaving about ten test compounds. Re-running that same routine across ten `random_state` values returns $R^2$ between **-0.09 and 0.82** and conformal quantiles between **1.97 and 4.92**. A metric computed from ten points describes the split, not the model, and the shipped figure happened to be a pessimistic draw.
    - `CalibratedQSARPredictor.evaluate_scaffold_cv` now supplies the reported numbers: 5 folds $\times$ 10 repeats of scaffold-grouped cross validation over 32 independent scaffolds, pooling 430 held-out predictions. On this dataset it gives RMSE 1.34 log units, $R^2$ 0.70, ROC AUC 0.95, Brier 0.10, conformal half width 3.12 log units.
    - The correction cuts **both** ways and the second direction matters more. The regressor carries more ordering signal than previously claimed, so the earlier statement that it "does not constrain potency at all" was too harsh. But a 3.12 log unit interval is still a factor of roughly 1300 on IC50, so the operative conclusion is unchanged: **rank ordering within the generated set is defensible, individual predicted IC50 values are not.**
    - Pinned by `tests/level4_oncology/test_qsar_validation.py`, which also asserts that the single-split routine remains unstable, so that nobody reverts to it while the dataset is this small.

12. **What Would Actually Improve Potency Estimation** (same study):
    - **More assay-homogeneous data is the highest value work.** A learning curve over scaffold-held-out folds shows RMSE falling from 2.04 to 1.04 log units as training scaffolds rise from 8 to 27. The curve is still descending steeply at the current size, so the estimator is data limited rather than architecture limited.
    - **Recalibrating the heuristic is not worth doing.** Refitting its intercept and slope against the curated drugs changes cross-validated RMSE from 2.34 to 2.48 (worse); refitting all four parameters reaches 2.20. Both remain far behind the random forest at 1.34. Widening the functional form to reach sub-micromolar references makes its range explode to physically meaningless values ($> 10^7\ \mu$M at the other end).
    - **The available fix is to stop using the heuristic for novel structures.** `predict_antitumor_activity` already has a calibrated-QSAR branch, but the tissue reporting path calls it without a `qsar_pred`, so that branch is dead code and the measurably worse heuristic is used instead. Wiring the existing model in would be a strict improvement on cross-validated accuracy. It is not done by default here because it changes every published tissue result and because a 3.12 log unit interval propagated into a tumour simulation still does not yield a defensible efficacy claim; the change should be made deliberately and the report regenerated, not slipped in.

13. **The Directed Search Barely Beats Greedy Hill Climbing** (measured 2026-10-28, `scripts/benchmark_generator.py`, output in `reports/generator_benchmark.json`):
    - On a shared objective (0.6 calibrated QSAR potency utility plus 0.4 medicinal chemistry plausibility), equal seed compounds and an equal budget of 300 objective evaluations over 3 repeats, the platform's NSGA-II search scores a top-10 mean of **0.7106** against hill climbing's **0.7093**. The margin is **+0.0013**, which is not a meaningful advantage.
    - Random retrieval from the local compound library achieves the single highest score of any strategy (0.7411) and by far the greatest chemical diversity (mean pairwise distance 0.887 against the directed search's 0.526). That baseline retrieves known drugs rather than generating anything, so its score is partly a statement about the objective; it is reported because "would picking from a catalogue have done as well" is a question a generative method should have to answer.
    - The directed search's advantages are real but narrow: chemical validity 0.96 against hill climbing's 0.60, and more unique scaffolds in its top set (4.3 against 2.3). It wastes far fewer evaluations on invalid structures and converges less brutally than greedy search.
    - **Consequence**: claims that the evolutionary machinery is doing substantial optimisation work are not supported at this budget on this objective. The observed structural convergence in long production runs, where a 20 generation search returned five gemcitabine analogues at Tanimoto 0.62 to 0.72, is consistent with this measurement.
    - This is an offline baseline comparison, not GuacaMol or MOSES. Those remain the right external benchmark and are listed in the roadmap.

14. **Medicinal Chemistry Filters Flag, They Do Not Delete** (`simulation/chemistry/medchem_filters.py`):
    - PAINS, Brenk and NIH substructure catalogues plus Ertl-Schuffenhauer synthetic accessibility are now computed for every candidate, and are available as a sixth search objective (`medchem_filters=True`).
    - Filtering is **off by default and scores rather than rejects**, because these rule sets have well documented false positives. Measured on this platform: aspirin trips Brenk, quercetin trips PAINS_B, Brenk and NIH, and paclitaxel, a pancreatic cancer standard of care, fails a strict filter on both alerts and synthetic accessibility (SA 5.92, 11 stereocentres, 62 heavy atoms). Hard rejection would discard approved drugs from a drug discovery platform.
    - Passing every filter means "not obviously implausible". It is not evidence that a compound can be synthesised, is stable, or does anything.

15. **Potency Uncertainty Can Now Be Propagated** (`simulation/pharmacology/uncertainty.py`):
    - The tissue model previously received a point IC50 and returned a point growth inhibition, presenting a prediction with a 1330 fold interval as definitely as a measurement.
    - Conformal prediction produces a **set with 95% marginal coverage, not a density**, so the module reports the range of tissue responses consistent with the interval and deliberately emits no mean, median or standard deviation over that band, which would invent a distribution shape the method never claimed.
    - Where the resulting band includes zero, the output states that the simulation does not establish that the compound does anything.

16. **The Molecule Now Decides the Entity's Fate** (`simulation/evolution/chemical_phenotype.py`, added 2026-10-28):
    - **The defect this removes.** The Mode A engine evolved four free-standing numbers, `catalytic_efficiency`, `stability_score`, `replication_threshold` and `decay_rate`, by adding Gaussian noise at each division. The molecular structure was mutated in parallel by RDKit, registered in the genealogy, and **never read back**. Measured directly: `simulation/evolution/darwinian.py` contained **zero** references to `canonical_smiles`, `mol_weight`, `logp` or `qed`. Replacing every structure with a random label would have changed nothing. There was no genotype to phenotype map, so the system was a numeric genetic algorithm with molecule names painted on it, and the description "chemical compounds as life forms" was not accurate.
    - **What replaced it.** Each survival trait is now derived deterministically from the structure with a stated chemical rationale: catalytic groups and polar surface set catalytic efficiency; aromatic and ring content raise stability while strained rings and hydrolysable linkages lower it; hydrolysable bond load sets the decay rate; heavy atom count and ring complexity set the cost of copying. Mutating the structure changes the traits; identical structures give identical traits.
    - **Demonstrated, not assumed.** With structural mutation and trait drift both disabled, so the phenotype map is the only difference between lineages, a hydrolysable anhydride population is eliminated completely (15 to 0) while a stable aromatic population grows (15 to 480) over 80 steps. Pinned by `tests/level3_evolution/test_chemical_phenotype.py`, which also asserts that disabling the map makes the two structures behave identically.
    - **What the map is not.** A heuristic, not biochemistry. No measured rate constants, no enzymology, no calibration against any experiment. It makes selection act on chemistry instead of on noise, which is a structural correctness property; it says nothing about real molecules.

17. **Population Regulation Is Weak, and Open-Endedness Is Not Demonstrated**:
    - The engine previously had no competition at all: a population of 40 reached 386 in 25 steps and exceeded 900 by step 33 with **zero deaths**. Without death there is no competition for selection to resolve.
    - A crowding term now divides available substrate by $1 + (N/N_{\text{ref}})^2$. It demonstrably throttles uptake, but in the tested regimes it does **not** cap the population, because reproduction is driven by banked energy rather than by uptake rate. The parameter is therefore named `crowding_reference` and not `carrying_capacity`: it sets the scale at which competition begins to bite, not a ceiling. Honest population regulation remains an open problem in this engine.
    - `simulation/evolution/openendedness.py` implements Bedau-Packard style activity statistics, which classify a run as class 1 (no adaptive activity), class 2 (bounded) or class 3 (unbounded). **The full test requires a neutral shadow model**, a matched population with inheritance but no selection, which is not implemented. A class 1 result from this tracker is therefore firm, since a system adopting no new components cannot be open ended under any shadow; a class 3 result is suggestive only and must not be reported as open-ended evolution.
    - Measured so far: the two-lineage competition run above classifies as **class 1**, diversity falling from 2 to 1. The system selects and then settles. No run in this repository has yet demonstrated sustained novelty.
